# Beluga character demo

Current scope: the user-approved version 5 base, colored and animated in the demo before clothing work continues.

- Approved geometry retained; colors applied to the surface.
- Head and flipper rig with two blink morph targets.
- Rotate, gentle idle movement, twirl, wiggle, kiss reaction, boop, saved looks, and photos.
- Hair and pearl color controls.
- Seven backgrounds, including Tuscany, with consistent panel geometry.
- Clothes and accessories are paused. Rejected wardrobe experiments are excluded from this demo.

The local Hunyuan3D reconstruction produced the approved base. Blender source is kept outside the static Site. three.js is vendored under dist/vendor with its license.
