# Sending the wardrobe

Play at [Belughina’s little wardrobe](https://talos91.github.io/belughina-wardrobe/).

The public [GitHub repository](https://github.com/Talos91/belughina-wardrobe) deploys automatically through GitHub Actions. The same HTTPS link works on a laptop or phone, with no account or installation.

## Included

- All 19 models, 17 clothing/accessory pieces, and the six new rooms.
- Direct category tabs, clear wearing indicators, a mobile expanded picker, and a labeled color sheet.
- Consistent top fits, a softened cream halter, a fitted black halter, and clean Moonlight lining rendering.
- Three held poses, crossed fins in Relaxed, four varied outfit reactions, and seamless returns after actions.
- Salami’s spawn/drop, rotating greeting lines, and the complete keepsake letter, permanently unlocked in that browser.
- Saved looks and photo export. Progress is local to each browser/device.

The main character downloads at 7.2 MiB. Other clothes and rooms load when selected. Full-quality Blender sources and original exports remain outside the publishing repository.

## Validation

Automated checks cover interrupted actions, held poses, paused frames, fin grounding, bottom silhouette consistency, garment attachments, 20 wrap combinations, Salami, and letter persistence. Browser checks cover phone portrait/landscape, colors and styling, the centered scroll letter, reload persistence, and the deployed site.

Physical iPhone/Pixel testing has not been performed. A quick cold load on each available device is useful before sending; if LINE or WhatsApp’s embedded browser struggles, open the same link in Safari or Chrome. Different browsers keep separate progress.
